## Brand & Style
This design system sets the visual standard for intelligent, carbon-conscious financial operations. It merges precision fintech telemetry with ambient environmental awareness, evoking surgical discipline, algorithmic speed, and ecological responsibility.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Carbon-Aware Glassmorphism**:
- Deep OLED obsidian and space-grade dark layers conserve power on emissive displays while establishing an infinite depth plane.
- Semi-translucent panels (`backdrop-blur`) establish spatial layering without visual weight.
- Controlled bioluminescent accents signal performance states: **Emerald Carbon** communicates vitality, efficiency, and revenue generation; **Electric Cyan & Indigo** drive AI inference and computational analytics; **Neon Amber** identifies volatile tolerances; and **Crimson** indicates outflow, resource spikes, or deficit risk.
- Minimal structural grid overlays and subtle ambient ray-tracing reflect precision computation without sensory fatigue.

## Colors
The system operates exclusively as a dark-first environment calibrated for reduced optical strain and display power optimization.

### Background Tiering
- **Canvas Base (`#07090E`)**: Infinite dark bedrock for global layouts and background viewports.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0B0F17`)**: Primary content base, card foundations, and navigational shells.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#111827`)**: Elevated containers, interactive table rows, and popovers.
- **Glass Tint (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)`)**: Used across translucent panels combined with background blur.

### Accents & Telemetry
- **Primary / Emerald Carbon (`#10B981` | Sub-tier `#059669`)**: Carbon-offset achievements, positive liquidity, sustainable yields, and verified ecological credits.
- **Secondary / Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**: Automated routing, AI predictive telemetry, and dynamic queries.
- **Tertiary / Quantum Indigo (`#6366F1`)**: Machine learning models, system optimization toggles, and analytical graphs.
- **Warning / Neon Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Carbon footprint approaching thresholds, compute usage surge, and budget warnings.
- **Negative / Rose Crimson (`#F43F5E`)**: Deficits, carbon-heavy investments, critical exceptions, and operational expenditures.

### High-Contrast Glass Borders
- Static edge highlight: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- Interactive/Active highlight: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)` or `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`

## Typography
Type is anchored on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, selected for its geometric clarity, crisp apex angles, and modern technical profile.

- **Tabular Numerals**: Numerical data across balance figures, ESG metrics, transaction streams, and AI telemetry strictly employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. This prevents layout shifts across real-time price updates.
- **Hierarchy & Contrast**: Headlines use tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale slate (`#F3F4F6`), creating high optical definition against deep backdrops. Body and descriptive metadata utilize cool secondary neutrals (`#9CA3AF` and `#6B7280`).
- **Telemetry Labels**: Badges, status markers, and operational indicators use uppercase treatment at `label-md` and `label-sm` with wider letter spacing (`0.04em` - `0.06em`) to ensure legibility across micro-screens.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid system calibrated to support rich data density without cognitive overload.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, 2rem section margin, 1.5rem gutter. Fixed navigation sidebar (280px) and auxiliary telemetry dock (320px) anchor the viewport.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, 1.5rem section margin, 1.25rem gutter. Auxiliary rails collapse into slide-out drawers or modal bottom panels.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout, 1rem margin, 1rem gutter. Metrics cards horizontally snap-scroll to preserve vertical canvas space for primary activity feeds.

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent multiples of 4px govern component internals. Information clusters remain tightly unified (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), panel padding relies on `space-md` to `space-lg`, and major structural boundaries leverage `space-xl` to maintain distinction between active cards.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved using translucent glass planes and ambient illumination instead of opaque dropshadows.

### The Glass Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor)**: Canvas background `#07090E`. Includes an ambient radial wash of Cyan/Emerald at 3% opacity positioned beneath high-priority charts.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Shells)**: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. No traditional shadow; defined cleanly by edge reflection.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces & Hover)**: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, and a diffused shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Palettes)**: `rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`, and an ambient edge-glow matched to the context (Emerald or Cyan at 15% opacity, blur radius 24px).

### Luminous Edge Highlights
Critical alerts and AI suggestions cast directional neon glows:
- **Carbon Surplus Glow**: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`
- **AI Processing Glow**: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`
- **Budget Threshold Glow**: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`
- **Over-Budget Warning Glow**: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)`

## Shapes
The structural language utilizes a controlled **Rounded** radius standard (`roundedness: 2`), balancing futuristic precision with ergonomic feel.

- **Base Radius (8px / 0.5rem)**: Standard buttons, text inputs, status chips, dropdown items, and table elements.
- **Large Radius (16px / 1rem - `rounded-lg`)**: Metric cards, modular dashboard widgets, notification banners, and modal containers.
- **XL Radius (24px / 1.5rem - `rounded-xl`)**: Primary system panels, sticky application docks, and hero financial visualizers.
- **Pill (Fully Rounded)**: Carbon-intensity badges, live node status markers, and micro-toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Emerald Glow**: Background `linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981 0%, #059669 100%)`, text `#FFFFFF` font-weight 600, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, box-shadow `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`. Active state compresses slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Cyber**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#F3F4F6`. Hover triggers `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`.
- **Destructive**: Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)`, text `#FDA4AF`. Hover yields a rose glow.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Built on `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `background: rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)` and `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Internal metrics employ tabular numerals with supporting unit labels. Top-right corners host clean energy status lights or trend badges.

### Clean Energy & Status Badges
- Compact pill-shaped elements (`rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`).
- **Eco Status (Clean Grid)**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#34D399`, left-aligned 6px pulsing emerald light.
- **AI Syncing**: Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`, text `#22D3EE`, border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
- **High Carbon Warning**: Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`, text `#FB7185`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `rgba(7, 9, 14, 0.7)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Height 42px, radius 8px, typography `body-sm`.
- Focus states apply zero browser rings; instead, they transition the border to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.8)` with a localized glow `0 0 12px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`. Placeholder text sits at `#4B5563`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 18px squares/circles, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked state transitions immediately to `#10B981` fill with white geometric checkmark or central bullseye pip, backed by a soft emerald point-light.

### Data Tables & Stream Lists
- Header cells: `label-sm` uppercase, text `#6B7280`, bottom border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Rows: Transparent background, transitioning on hover to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`. Cell divider uses faint hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`). All financial amounts and carbon metrics align flush right with tabular numbers.
